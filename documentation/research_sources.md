## Source Usage Notes

S01: Used to structure AI governance, risk identification,
measurement, and management.

S02: Used to evaluate Nigerian data protection requirements,
including lawful processing, individual rights, and relevant
automated decision-making provisions.

S03: Used to assess the potential applicability of European
AI regulatory requirements, depending on the organisation's
activities and territorial scope.

S04: Used to evaluate human rights, fairness, transparency,
privacy, and ethical governance.

S05: Used to evaluate accountability, robustness, transparency,
human-centred values, and responsible AI practices.
